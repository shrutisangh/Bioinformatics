# 01 · Literature Search: PubMed and Google Scholar

## Aim
To search scientific literature on one topic (iron) in PubMed and Google Scholar, record the bibliographic details of articles, generate citations in APA and AMA formats, and compare the two databases.

## Tools

## Results
A keyword search for *iron* returned 314,699 PubMed records. Two records were studied in detail and cited in APA and AMA styles. Google Scholar returned roughly 16 times more results than PubMed because it indexes every discipline and many source types.

## Conclusion
PubMed gives focused, curated biomedical results with MeSH filtering and PMIDs, so it is best for clinical and life-science questions. Google Scholar gives broad coverage and a *Cited by* link for every item but has less precise filtering. Using both together, and exporting citations in the style required by the journal, gives a complete and properly referenced literature search.

## Notes for review
- The AMA description in the journal (a style widely used in the social and behavioral sciences) actually describes **APA**. **AMA** is the American Medical Association style used in medicine and biomedical journals.
- The first table originally listed a URL in the *Journal Name* column. The journal is JAMA; the URL is the article link.
- PubMed does not index patents, and Google Scholar does not assign PMIDs. Table 4 shows those two cells accordingly; check them against your own observations.
